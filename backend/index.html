Possible explanations · not a diagnosis or prescription
How it works

AI reads your full description, plans medical reference searches, then compares possible explanations using MedlinePlus excerpts. Medication names and evidence quotes are checked against their cited excerpts. Check original sources; AI can make mistakes.

There is no physical exam, validated triage, or complete medication-interaction check. Treatment needs clinician or pharmacist review.

Privacy & limitations

AI reasoning sends your full description and retrieved excerpts to OpenAI when enabled. AI-generated medical topic searches go to NLM services; without AI, your question is sent directly to MedlinePlus. Avoid identifying information.

Prompts are not intentionally saved by this app. Service and browser logs may retain data. Sources and generated text are for education, not diagnosis or medical decisions.
