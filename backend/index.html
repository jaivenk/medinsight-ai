Using these references

Health-topic results are educational resources, not diagnoses. The system does not confirm whether a fictional case has a particular condition.

Privacy and safety: This local prototype has no account storage or automatic patient-record backup implemented. Submitted form data is processed by your local FastAPI server; searches send search terms to MedlinePlus. Browser, OS, developer tools, or server logs may still retain information. Do not enter real patient details or rely on results for medical decisions.
